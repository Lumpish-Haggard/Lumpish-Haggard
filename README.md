## About me

I’m the Co-Founder and CTO of [Atrevion Private Limited](https://github.com/AtrevionPVT-LTD), where I lead product direction, technical strategy, and engineering execution.

At Atrevion, I’m building **Atreya** — a healthcare management platform for Indian clinics that brings together clinic operations, patient management, scheduling, billing, pharmacy inventory, HR workflows, ABDM integrations, and AI-assisted clinical documentation.

I also work on **AtrevionScribe**, an offline, multilingual medical audio-processing system that uses speaker diarization, speech recognition, translation, normalization, and language models to convert doctor–patient conversations into structured SOAP notes. My work includes designing model pipelines, evaluating different ASR and translation approaches, and building systems that can run with privacy-sensitive healthcare data locally.

## What I’m building at Atrevion

### Atreya
A multi-tenant healthcare management platform combining HMS, HRMS, pharmacy inventory, clinic operations, ABDM connectivity, and AI-assisted consultation workflows.

**Core technologies:** Java, Spring Boot, PostgreSQL, Flutter, Docker, Firebase, WebSockets, Flyway, and REST APIs.

### AtrevionScribe
A fully offline medical speech pipeline for multilingual consultations.

**Pipeline:** Audio → speaker diarization → ASR → translation → normalization → structured SOAP note.

The project explores Whisper, Indic ASR, IndicTrans2, Llama, Gemma, pyannote, and other specialized models while prioritizing local processing and healthcare data privacy.

### Client and product engineering
I also lead technology work on client-focused products such as **CARE**, a configurable management-system foundation with Flutter, Riverpod, role-based access control, dynamic modules, reusable design systems, and cloud infrastructure.
